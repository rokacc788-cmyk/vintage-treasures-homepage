# Vintage Treasures Homepage

Create a professional website homepage for a small independent antique, vintage and second-hand shop in the UK.

Use a warm, atmospheric vintage style: dark wood, cream, subtle brass/gold details and elegant typography. Make it feel unique, welcoming and slightly mysterious, like discovering a hidden antique shop. Keep it clean and modern enough to look professional.

At the top, create a proper header with space for the shop logo on the left and a navigation bar: Home | What We Sell | About | Contact. Also include small social icons for Facebook and eBay.

Add a large hero section with a shop photo, shop name, short welcoming message and Explore the Shop button.

Add a What We Sell preview showing categories such as Cameras & Photography, Vintage Clothing, Binoculars & Optics, Furniture, Antiques & Collectibles, Vintage Electronics and Unusual Finds.

Add a Meet the Owner section with space for the owner's photo and personal introduction.

Add a Visit Us section with address, opening hours, contact details and directions.

Add a professional footer/toolbox containing the logo, navigation links, contact details, Facebook, eBay, opening hours and copyright.

This is NOT an online store. No prices, cart, checkout or product listings. Customers visit the physical shop or use eBay/Facebook for online purchases.

Make it mobile-friendly, highly visual and easy to navigate. Use image placeholders that I can replace with real shop photos later.
